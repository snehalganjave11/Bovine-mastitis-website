ABOUT PROJECT

Early Detection of Bovine Mastitis

Bovine mastitis is an inflammatory disease of the udder that can reduce milk production and affect the health of dairy cattle.

Our proposed AI-based system analyzes important parameters collected from dairy cattle and predicts the possibility of mastitis before severe symptoms appear.

Early forecasting can help Indian dairy farmers take timely preventive action, improve animal health and reduce financial losses.

🎯 Main Objective

To develop an affordable and intelligent early warning system for bovine mastitis suitable for Indian dairy farms.
